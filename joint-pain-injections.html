What Are Joint Pain Injections?

Joint pain injections are minimally invasive procedures used to treat pain in joints such as the knee, shoulder, hip, and spine. These injections deliver medication directly into the affected joint for targeted relief.

They are commonly used for conditions like arthritis, inflammation, sports injuries, and degenerative joint disorders.

The procedure is performed under imaging guidance to ensure precise placement and maximum effectiveness.

Conditions Treated

  • Knee pain and osteoarthritis
  • Shoulder pain and frozen shoulder
  • Hip joint pain
  • Spine-related pain
  • Sports injuries
  • Inflammatory joint conditions

Types of Joint Injections

  • Steroid Injections: Reduce inflammation and pain
  • Hyaluronic Acid Injections: Improve joint lubrication
  • PRP Therapy: Promotes natural healing
  • Nerve Block Injections: Target pain signals

Procedure Overview

  • Performed under ultrasound or imaging guidance
  • Local anesthesia is administered
  • Medication is injected precisely into the joint
  • Quick procedure with minimal discomfort

Patients can usually return home the same day and resume normal activities shortly after.
