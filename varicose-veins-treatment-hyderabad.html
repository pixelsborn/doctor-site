What Are Varicose Veins?

Varicose veins are enlarged, twisted veins filled with blood, commonly seen in the legs and feet. They occur when the valves in the veins become weak or damaged, causing blood to flow backward and accumulate.

These veins may appear dark blue or purple and are often visible under the skin. In some cases, they are accompanied by spider veins, which are smaller and closer to the skin surface.

Symptoms of Varicose Veins

Some patients may not experience discomfort, but common symptoms include:

  • Swollen, twisted, visible veins
  • Dark purple or blue discoloration
  • Heavy or aching legs
  • Burning or throbbing sensation
  • Muscle cramps, especially at night
  • Swelling in lower legs or ankles
  • Itching around veins
  • Skin changes or ulcers (in severe cases)

Causes & Risk Factors

Varicose veins develop when vein valves fail to function properly, leading to poor blood circulation.

  • Age above 50
  • Prolonged standing or sitting
  • Obesity
  • Pregnancy and hormonal changes
  • Family history (genetics)
  • Menopause

Treatment Options

Self-Care & Lifestyle
  • Regular exercise
  • Weight management
  • Elevating legs
  • Avoid prolonged sitting/standing
Medical Treatment
  • Compression stockings
  • Sclerotherapy (injection treatment)
  • Foam sclerotherapy
  • Laser treatment
  • Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA)

These modern treatments are minimally invasive and highly effective in treating varicose veins.
